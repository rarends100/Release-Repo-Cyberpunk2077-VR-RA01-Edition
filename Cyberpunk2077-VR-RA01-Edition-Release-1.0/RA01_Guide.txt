# A line starting with "## " is a card title; the lines under it are that card. Lines starting with "#" (one hash and a space) are notes and are not shown.
## Settings window
Open it: X, then LT, then right stick LEFT (or press F10). Point and pull the trigger to click; the sticks scroll it.
Right stick UP in the same chord opens the game menu (inventory, map, journal); DOWN opens the pause menu.
Every feature below can be switched off or tuned in that window. Hover a row for a full explanation.
## Moving
Walk with the left stick, turn with the right stick or your body. Squat for real to crouch.
Ladders: go up to the ladder, get on it, then squeeze a grip and pull that hand down to climb. Water: butterfly stroke or doggy paddle to swim forward, side sweeping motions to turn in water, look down to dive.
## Guns
Point the gun itself - shots follow the barrel. A laser shows where it hits.
Raise the gun to your cheek to aim (no zoom). Reach your other hand to the gun and squeeze its grip to hold it with two hands; tuck the back of the gun to your shoulder for a steady stock.
## Reloading
Hold X for about half a second to drop the magazine (a quick press is still doors and loot), or hold your off-hand trigger on the magazine and pull it out. Reach to your off-hand hip (the glowing ball), squeeze to take a new one, push it into the small ball under the gun.
## Melee, Monowire, power attacks
Swing fast to strike; hold a guard pose in front of your face to block.
Pull a hand back behind your shoulder, hold a moment, then swing for a strong attack. Both hands up and slam down with Gorilla Arms.
Monowire: choose the X or Straight look on the Weapons tab. Charging RT keeps it between your hands; it whips out when the attack fires.
## Grenades and knives
Hold the grip to take a grenade out, swing, and let go of the grip to throw - it lands where a real throw would. No aiming arc in the way.
Knives: hold to aim, then swing the knife hand forward hard to throw.
## Driving
Put a hand on the wheel or handlebar, squeeze the grip to hold it, and steer. X is the horn.
## Gestures
open the weapons wheel, choose hands, then Hold B for 5 seconds to switch gesture mode on or off: grip = point, trigger = thumbs up, both = the middle (fuck you) finger.
## First start
Run this launcher, pick your settings, press LAUNCH. Keep this window open while you play - when the game closes it puts everything back.
On the first start with a new headset, press "Put camera at V's eyes" and "Calibrate arms" in the settings window.
